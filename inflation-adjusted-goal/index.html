What will your goal cost after inflation?

A goal stated in today's money is not the amount you will need when you spend it. Prices rise, so the same lifestyle costs more in future rupees. Ignoring that is the most common planning error.

This page converts goals into their future cost at different inflation rates, then shows the monthly SIP needed to reach the inflated amount at 12%.

At 6% inflation, prices roughly double every 12 years. The table inflates today's goal to the amount you would need at the point of spending.

Frequently asked questions

What inflation rate should I use?

India's long-run CPI inflation has often run around 5-7%. Using 6% is a reasonable planning default; the goal planner lets you change it.

Should I plan in today's money or future money?

Either, consistently. Planning in today's money uses a real return (return minus inflation), which is simpler. Planning in future money inflates the goal - which is what this page does.

Does inflation affect a home loan goal?

Yes - the down payment for a house will cost more in the future too, so inflate the target the same way before you work out the SIP.
