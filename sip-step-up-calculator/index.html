SIP step-up calculator: how much does raising your SIP each year add?

A step-up SIP raises your monthly contribution by a fixed percentage every year. It matches the way incomes usually grow, and over long horizons the small annual raises compound into a much larger corpus.

₹10,000 a month outgrows many people's budgets within a few years; a step-up lets you start at a comfortable level and grow into the target. The tables show the effect at 5%, 10% and 15%.

Frequently asked questions

Is a step-up better than a higher starting SIP?

For the same total money, a higher flat SIP usually wins because the extra rupees are invested sooner. A step-up wins when you cannot afford the higher amount today but expect your income to grow.

How often should I step up?

Annually is easiest to automate and matches most salary cycles. Even 5% a year makes a visible difference over 15-20 years.

Can I step up and also invest a lump sum?

Yes. The SIP strategy lab and what-if lab let you combine a lump sum, a monthly SIP and a step-up in one comparison.
